🛰 Orbit Academy

An interactive course in orbital dynamics for non-specialists — eight modules, each a guided worksheet plus a live 3-D simulator. Runs completely offline. Current release: v5.5.

Just the course  (recommended for learners)

All eight modules, simulators, and interactive quizzes. Nothing to install — unzip it and open START-HERE.html. Progress is saved in your browser. No internet connection is ever needed.

⬇ Download the course (zip, ~15 MB)

Course + classroom management  (for instructors)

Everything above, plus a sign-on server: student accounts, saved progress on any machine, modules unlocking in order, an instructor dashboard with class analytics, and a worksheet editor. Needs one free install (Node.js); the included START-HERE page walks you through it.

⬇ Download course + management (zip, ~16 MB)

Printable worksheets (PDF)

Paper companions to the course — objectives, tutorial, every exercise and quiz question. The interactive worksheets in the course download are the real thing (live simulators, self-checking quizzes); use these only where a screen isn't an option or for marked-up review.
