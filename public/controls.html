Simulator Controls

One reference for every Orbit Academy module — you’ll use these throughout.
The 3-D simulator uses the same navigation and time controls in every tutorial. Learn them once here, then keep this page handy (every worksheet’s header has a 🃏 Cheat sheet button with a quick-reference card).

🧭 Move the camera

Click + drag
Pan — slide the view around.
Right-drag / Shift+drag
Rotate — orbit the camera around the scene.
Arrow keys ←→↑↓
Rotate — same as right-drag, from the keyboard.
Scroll wheel / pinch
Zoom in and out.
Two-finger drag
Pan (trackpad).
R
Reset view — snap the camera back to the default.

⏱ Control time

. (period)
Speed up time — orbits move faster.
, (comma)
Slow down time — all the way down to 1⁄32 speed for a close look.
Top-left readout
Shows the current speed-up vs. real time (e.g. “3 hr/sec”). Orbits are always sped up so you can see them.

🛰 Explore & inject

Checkboxes (left)
Turn real constellations on/off (ISS, Starlink, GPS, GEO, demos).
Altitude / speed / inclination sliders
Set up your own object, then Inject it and watch its orbit.
Click a satellite
Select an object you injected to read its orbital details on the right (the built-in constellation satellites aren’t clickable).
Tip: keep the simulator window and your worksheet side by side. Do what each task says in the sim, then answer its check question in the worksheet.
