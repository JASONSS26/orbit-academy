📡 TLE of your orbit
The same six numbers, written the way NORAD publishes them — a Two-Line Element set. This is what a ground station downloads to track your satellite.
—
The mean anomaly field is your “position” slider re-expressed as an average angle, and mean motion (orbits per day) is just another way of stating the period. Notice: moving RAAN or argument of perigee changes the TLE, but not the mean motion — same size, same period.
