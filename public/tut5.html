⚖ Release on the Earth–Moon line
Drop an object at rest somewhere between Earth and Moon. Which way does it fall? The tipping point is the balance point.
release point50% toward Moon
balance
The balance point is about 85% of the way to the Moon (not the midpoint — the Moon is much lighter, so its gravitational “territory” is small). Inside 85% → falls back toward Earth; beyond → falls onto the Moon.

Cislunar zones
xGEO = “extended GEO”: farther from Earth than the GEO belt (~36,000 km) but still inside Earth’s gravitational grip — out to the edge of Earth’s Hill sphere, ~1.5 million km. The Moon, at 384,400 km, sits squarely in this region.
