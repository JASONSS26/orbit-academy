This scenario
L1–L5 are the five spots where, under Earth + Moon together, an object keeps the same 27.3-day period as the Moon and so holds station on the rotating Earth–Moon line. L1 and L2 are unstable — like a golf ball balanced on a basketball — so real missions there fly small looping halo orbits and nudge often.
